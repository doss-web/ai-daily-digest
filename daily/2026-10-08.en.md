## 📄 Papers

**📄 WebFovea: When the model is right but the click is wrong — reliable round trips for vision-based web agents**
- **What it did**: The authors present WebFovea, a vision-based web agent that placed 2nd in the WebRetriever Challenge 2026 with a final score of 57.0/100 on Protocol III. The paper's central claim: a capable multimodal LLM is necessary but not sufficient — the model's decisions must pass through the browser layer to land, and "where the click lands" is the real bottleneck.
- **Why it matters**: Every team building browser-use/computer-use agents is stuck in the same place: the plan is right, the execution coordinates are off. This paper offers a quantifiable end-to-end evaluation protocol rather than another capability boast.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03036)

**📄 Structuring MoE Expert Selection for Agentic Reinforcement Learning**
- **What it did**: The paper systematically studies the connection between agentic post-training and MoE expert selection. The observation is concrete: in off-the-shelf MoE models, expert routing already exhibits a specialized structure that naturally aligns with agentic trajectories — expert routing overlaps more between adjacent agent turns.
- **Why it matters**: If your agent runs on a sparse model, this result means MoE routing structure can be directly exploited — even shaped — by agentic RL rather than treated as a black box. For teams optimizing inference cost, it turns sparsity from a "save compute" lever into a "align behavior" lever.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.07332)

**📄 TimeBraid: Unifying time series and language for understanding and forecasting**
- **What it did**: TimeBraid aligns pretrained language models and pretrained time-series foundation models through interleaved global residual attention layers. The result is a family of models that inherits instruction following and reasoning from the language side, and continuous-signal perception and zero-shot forecasting from the time-series side, fusing both in a shared representation space where both modalities are understood and generated.
- **Why it matters**: Time-series forecasting has long been trapped in islands of specialist models, unable to access LLM reasoning or tool use. This "align two pretrained models" rather than "train multimodal from scratch" route is far more reproducible for teams with limited compute.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29792)

**📄 Sensor-Language-Action Models: sensors shouldn't just perceive the world, they should decide the next move**
- **What it did**: Existing sensor models largely stop at perception — recognizing states or predicting outcomes — while actions are modeled separately through task-specific, often closed-label-space models. The authors propose SLA (Sensor-Language-Action), using language as a semantic interface between sensor observations and actions within a unified model.
- **Why it matters**: This is an attempt to eliminate the seam between perception and decision. For teams working on embodied AI or industrial IoT, language as an interface means adding a sensor or an action no longer requires retraining the whole model — the cost structure of extension changes.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.08244)

**📄 DAEDALUS: Bootstrapping agent memory from self-generated tasks**
- **What it did**: LLM agents lack the operational knowledge to act reliably in new environments — they must discover tool behaviors and environment conventions on their own. Without memory of past attempts they repeat the same mistakes across tasks, causing more failures and longer trajectories. Existing approaches rely on human-written guidelines or procedural memory built from training tasks and an oracle verifier — both require prior knowledge of the environment. DAEDALUS bootstraps this process from self-generated tasks.
- **Why it matters**: If agents can grow their own memory in environments with no human guidance and no oracle, the upfront cost of "deploying into a new environment" drops sharply. For teams delivering enterprise agents, that maps directly onto implementation cycle time.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.08048)
