**🔥 Alibaba releases Qwen-Drive 1.0: the model can explain why it brakes — but the explanation doesn't match the maneuver**
- **What happened**: Alibaba's research team released Qwen-Drive 1.0, unifying environmental perception, traffic Q&A, and route planning in a single model intended to run both the cockpit and the driving system. A key finding: text-image pretraining does not automatically produce 3D spatial understanding — spatial awareness must be explicitly trained. Evaluations also show the model's explanations of its own braking behavior frequently mismatch the actual maneuver.
- **Why it matters**: End-to-end driving is one of the hardest embodied-AI deployments, and the "two-layer" problem of decision vs. explanation directly undermines regulator and passenger trust. Teams working on autonomous driving or embodied models should treat explainability as a separately trained capability, not an emergent byproduct.
