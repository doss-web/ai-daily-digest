- **What happened**: The paper finds SFT and RL behave fundamentally differently in enhancing multi-task reasoning: SFT suffers severe task conflicts under multi-stage training, while RL enables stable coexistence across diverse tasks. - **Why it matters**: Directly relevant to designing multi-task training pipelines. It provides theoretical explanation and experimental evidence for why SFT tasks interfere with each other while RL tasks don't — practical guidance for anyone doing model fine-tuning.

**📄 Round-Trip Consistency: Diffusion Models Can Predict Their Own Rollout Errors**
- **What happened**: The paper trains a single conditional latent diffusion model that steps a dynamical system forward or backward via a direction flag. Key finding: rolling forward i steps then backward i steps must return to the start, so the round-trip discrepancy serves as a label-free test-time error signal.
- **Why it matters**: Autoregressive models accumulate error over long rollouts with no ground truth to measure against at deployment. This method provides a ground-truth-free error estimate, with direct applications to world models, simulation, and robot policy deployment.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.00675)

**📄 Zero Gap Is Not Restoration: A New Approach to Benchmark Contamination Mitigation Evaluation**
- **What happened**: The paper shows the prevailing contamination-mitigation metric G-AP is flawed — discrete correct/incorrect readouts cannot characterize per-question performance, and averaging before differencing lets over- and under-suppression cancel out. - **Why it matters**: Benchmark contamination is the #1 problem in evaluating LLMs' true capability. This paper offers a more reliable evaluation framework — relevant to any team publishing benchmark scores.
