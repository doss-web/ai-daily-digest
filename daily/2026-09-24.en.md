**🔥 Meta's Muse ships with a 0-day that could fully hijack a victim's Mac**
- **What happened**: Wired and Ars Technica reported a serious zero-day in Muse that would let attackers do "whatever" they wanted on a victim's Mac via a ClickFix-style attack. Meta says it issued a fix. Ars Technica's framing is telling: Muse is an extraordinarily privileged AI assistant, and the problem lies precisely in its permission scope.
- **Why it matters**: This is a demonstration of a generic architectural risk for agent products — when the assistant has shell, filesystem and network access at once, one input-side nudge equals full remote takeover. Design agent permission models with this as the default threat model: least privilege, operation confirmation and rollback are the three must-haves.
- **Connections**: Together with the Australian Medicare incident, Microsoft's EvilTokens takedown and the MCP agent-hijacking paper, this forms four agent-security evidence lines on a single day.
