**📄 β-OPSD: Deriving with Policy Optimization, Training with Self-Distillation**
- **What happened**: The paper shows that on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) is precisely the β=1 member of a broader policy-optimization family, where β weights the KL penalty anchoring the student to a reference policy. Turning β from an implicit constant into a controllable regularizer materially improves training stability, turning inference-model distillation from "fragile art" into a tunable optimization problem.
- **Why it matters**: For teams doing post-training on reasoning models, this is directly applicable: viewing OPSD as policy optimization with a tunable β explains previously mysterious failure modes (same config, sometimes works, sometimes not) and provides a one-dimensional stabilization knob.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.28582)
