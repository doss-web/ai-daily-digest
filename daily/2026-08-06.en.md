## 📄 Papers

**📄 Know When to Stop: Segment-Level Credit Assignment for Reducing Overthinking**
- **What happened**: Reasoning models' "overthinking" problem — generating lengthy hedges, abandoned approaches, and self-contradictions that burn tokens — has long been framed as a length problem. This paper shows that even controlling for response length, incorrect traces exhibit significantly higher rates of unproductive self-reflection. The authors propose segment-level credit assignment to identify when self-reflection helps vs. hurts, enabling timely termination of unproductive thinking during generation.
- **Why it matters**: Token cost is one of the biggest bottlenecks for deploying reasoning models. This research offers an actionable methodology for "knowing when to stop," directly relevant to reasoning API cost optimization.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.00482)

**📄 Test-Time Scaling in Reasoning LLMs: Inference Regimes, Evaluation, and Reproducibility**
- **What happened**: As inference-time compute has become a primary lever for improving LLM reasoning, "test-time scaling" now covers multiple distinct algorithm families: extending deliberation along a single trajectory, sampling complete candidates and aggregating via voting/verification, and searching over unfinished partial states. The paper argues these algorithms differ fundamentally in statistical structure, compute accounting, and failure modes, and proposes a unified evaluation framework.
- **Why it matters**: Most current test-time scaling comparisons conflate different reasoning regimes, making conclusions non-transferable. This survey + unified framework is essential reading for reasoning cost optimization and model selection.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04001v1)

**📄 LLaDA MoE v2: Scaling Mixture-of-Experts Diffusion Language Models**
- **What happened**: Diffusion language models (dLLMs) are positioned as an alternative to autoregressive models, but their MoE scaling behavior had not been systematically studied. This paper characterizes how optimization hyperparameters, compute allocation, and architecture scale for MoE dLLMs, finding quantitative differences from autoregressive scaling trends — optimal nominal batch size grows faster, and optimal learning rate decay behaves differently.
- **Why it matters**: If diffusion LMs are to scale up, these scaling laws directly determine training cost and architecture choices. Valuable for teams tracking non-autoregressive paradigms.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.03457)

**📄 CALVER: When Voting Fails, Symbolic Verification for Causal Reasoning**
- **What happened**: Self-consistency assumes the most frequent answer among sampled reasoning traces is most reliable, but in causal reasoning this assumption often fails: samples repeat the same confounding error, and valid answers fragment across multiple candidates, letting an invalid answer win. The paper introduces CALVER, a training-free symbolic verifier that scores structured traces against Pearl's causal criteria (d-separation, backdoor adjustment, etc.).
- **Why it matters**: Voting/sampling aggregation is one of the most popular reasoning enhancement techniques, but the paper reveals its systematic failure in causal scenarios. CALVER offers a training-free method to improve reasoning reliability, with direct application to agent decision-making.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.03506)

**📄 ARCHead: Activation-Metric Residual Correction for LM-Head Compression**
- **What happened**: Weight-only quantization substantially compresses LLM transformer blocks, but practical backends often keep the LM-head in BF16/FP16, making it a memory bottleneck. ARCHead proposes a packaged compressor combining a quantized low-rank core, group-wise INT4 residuals, and a low-rank correction fitted in an activation-derived metric — no dense weights needed to restore the vocabulary logit distribution.
- **Why it matters**: For teams deploying very large models, LM-head compression is an under-explored optimization space. ARCHead may open a new memory-saving path beyond KV cache quantization.
- Importance: ★★★☆☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.02703)
