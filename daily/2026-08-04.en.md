**📄 From RLVR to RLSVR: task transformation induces self-verifiable rewards for open-ended tasks**
- **What happened**: RLVR is limited to deterministically verifiable domains like math and code. This paper proposes transforming open-ended tasks into verifiable forms, inducing self-verifiable rewards so LLMs can self-improve in writing, creative, and research domains.
- **Why it matters**: If self-verifiable rewards hold, RL training in open domains no longer depends on reward models and LLM judges, cutting evaluation bias and inference cost. A pivotal step toward extending RLVR beyond math and code.
- **Connections**: Alongside SAF-OPD and β-OPSD, this is part of a fast-converging research line fusing RLVR with on-policy self-distillation.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.23802)

**📄 Mental World Modeling: predicting human behavior requires modeling mental states**
- **What happened**: Existing world models answer only physical questions—what/where objects are and how they evolve. Human behavior is driven by hidden mental states (beliefs, desires, intentions, feelings, social permissibility). The authors formalize Mental World Modeling (MWM), arguing a model that tracks the physical scene but not what each agent knows and believes will predict the wrong action for a right-looking scene.
- **Why it matters**: Extending world models from physics to psychology is the necessary path from "physical simulators" to "human behavior simulators." A direct methodological update for agent-collaboration and social-simulation researchers.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.27201)

**📄 RL²-VLA: test-time scaling for Vision-Language-Action models**
- **What happened**: VLA models degrade on out-of-domain and difficult tasks. This paper proposes adaptive RL latent compositional steering with test-time scaling, letting action sampling escape correlated failure modes where samples cluster around similar behaviors—without heavy data collection or retraining.
- **Why it matters**: Test-time scaling is moving from LLMs to embodied AI. Robotics is about to meet the same paradigm as LLMs—more inference compute, higher task success. Teams building robot policies should track this line.
- Importance: ★★★★☆
- Source: [HuggingFace Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.26991)

**📄 Safeguards based on copyable context cannot provide reliable safety for LLMs**
