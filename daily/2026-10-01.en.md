**📄 AgentTell: Behavioural Side-Channel Leakage in Browser-Use Agents**
- **What happened**: The paper points out that browser-use agents carry information in their context as they move between websites. For example, after an agent reads a membership record and learns a user's affiliation, it may then select an affiliation-specific registration option on another site instead of the general one — leaking that private fact. The leak happens at the level of behavioral patterns rather than text output. The work systematically characterizes this class of behavioral side channel.
- **Why it matters**: Almost all existing agent privacy defenses focus on "don't say the sensitive thing," but this paper shows the real leak path is "which button gets clicked." Teams building browser agents or automated form-filling need to build behavioral consistency — not just output filtering — into their privacy design.
- **Connections**: Belongs to the same fast-forming agent-security direction as SEAD and IEEE Spectrum's reporting on covert agent collaboration.
- Importance: ★★★★★
- Source: [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.32915)
