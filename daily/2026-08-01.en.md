**🔥 Google Earth's AI satellite-image generation feature pulled after one day**
- **What happened**: Google Earth launched an AI editing feature allowing users to superimpose generated content onto real satellite imagery via text prompts — e.g., "refugees near the Mexican border" or "a bomb crater near a hospital in Gaza." Examples generated by digital investigator Henk van Ess triggered immediate backlash, and Google removed the feature within 24 hours, saying it "didn't meet the bar for intended use." No timeline was given for a possible return.

For any team integrating generative AI into fact-based products (maps, news, healthcare, legal), this is a warning: generation and truthfulness constraints must be designed together, not patched afterward.
