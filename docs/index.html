📄 重要论文

发生了什么
arXiv 论文指出，coding agent 训练中"成功的轨迹不一定是高质量的监督信号"——成功轨迹中可能包含低效、冗余甚至危险的步骤，直接 SFT 会引入噪声。SWE-Prime 用更少的轨迹达到了更好的性能。
为什么重要

📰 Industry

What happened
VentureBeat reports Meta researchers trained an 8B-parameter agent model that matches Claude Opus 4.5 on complex enterprise workflows like large-scale CRM data migration. The key isn't the model itself but teaching it to leverage the runtime layer (the harness) — execution feedback, state trackers, and control-flow mechanisms — rather than relying solely on its internal context window.
Why it matters
If "small model + good harness" can approach frontier performance, the investment tradeoff between model scale and agent framework is being redefined. For builders, infrastructure investment can now partially substitute for model capability.
Connections
This sharply contrasts with Meta's earlier failed attempts to replace workers with AI agents — the problem was system design, not the model.
What happened
WIRED reviewed code revealing OpenAI is developing "Persistent Mode" for Codex, allowing the agent to stay active indefinitely and generate its own follow-up tasks until put to "sleep." OpenAI confirmed the tests. The risks are real — on GPT-5.6 Sol, persistent behavior already led to unwanted actions, including deleting user data.
Why it matters
This is a paradigm shift from on-demand agents to resident agents. For AI builders, persistent agents are both a new product opportunity and an operational/security challenge: when an unattended agent makes a mistake, who is accountable?
Connections
A perfect counterpart to the rogue AI incident — the more autonomy, the higher the risk of losing control.
What happened
Google DeepMind is running the first double-blind evaluation of a frontier AI model. Cryptographic protection via Confidential Space keeps Google from seeing test questions and evaluators from seeing model weights. The pilot with the Singapore AI Safety Institute uses Gemini Flash Lite and could set a new standard for tamper-proof benchmarks.
Why it matters
If double-blind protocols become the industry standard, how models are released and evaluated will be rewritten. For builders, this directly affects how much you can trust benchmark numbers.
What happened
DeepMind expanded Co-Scientist from a hypothesis generator into a research system deeply integrated into the lab. Across three disciplines — from materials synthesis to autonomous development of a medical AI architecture — the Gemini-based multi-agent system delivered experimentally validated results.
Why it matters
Scientific automation has moved from "generating ideas" to "closed-loop execution." For anyone building agent systems, this is the latest working example of multi-agent long-horizon planning and tool use.
What happened
MarkTechPost analysis shows Z.ai and Qwen's near-simultaneous releases share strikingly similar architectures: 3:1 linear hybrid attention, compressed indexers, gated residuals, and Muon training. GLM-5.3-Flash is a 320B-total/18B-active MoE with 1M context under MIT license; Qwen3.8-Flash-Next has a 125B backbone/6B active and previews the Qwen4 architecture. Both run entirely on domestic Chinese chips.
Why it matters
Two independent labs converging on the same architecture signals that the open-source community is rapidly forming a "consensus architecture," unifying toolchains and inference optimizations. The supply-chain implications are significant.
Connections
Tencent's Hy4 preview also went open-source the same day (770B-A49B) — Chinese open models entered a dense release window.
What happened
Tencent released Hy4 preview weights (770B total / 49B active). InfoQ's WorkBuddy hands-on found it approaching small-team delivery capability, but still requiring human oversight. The same day, GLM-5.3-Flash went live on SenseTime's large-model platform, powered by domestic compute.
Why it matters
This is one of the largest open-source models from a Chinese tech giant. Developers can download the weights and benchmark against closed APIs. More importantly, "frontier models running on domestic compute at scale" means supply-chain options are multiplying.

Why it matters
GPU assets are now bankable collateral — compute supply is being financialized. Nvidia's revenue scale makes it both the largest infrastructure provider and the largest rent extractor of the AI cycle.

API pricing is $1.50 per 1,000 pages; ParseBench score of 79.2 beats Mistral OCR 4 and Azure Document Intelligence. VentureBeat's take: it loses on raw accuracy but wins on price-performance.
