发生了什么

📄 重要论文

发生了什么
论文指出现有全模态模型的 token 压缩常由单一模态决定保留内容，这假设在多模态场景下经常失效——同一查询下，音频和视频的相关性峰值往往出现在不同时刻。OmniScope 以 query 为锚点，对各模态做独立压缩，无需训练即可用。
为什么重要
长视频/多模态上下文的成本瓶颈在 token 数。OmniScope 这种训练无关的压缩方法可以直接嵌入现有推理管线，是工程上低成本见效的方向。
★★★★★
HuggingFace
发生了什么
论文识别出 on-policy self-distillation（OPSD）在实践中的脆弱性源于一个结构性问题：vanilla OPSD 恰是更广策略优化家族中 β=1 的特例，其中 β 控制锚定到参考策略的 KL 惩罚权重。作者将 β 从隐式固定值变为可控正则项，显著提升训练稳定性。
为什么重要
推理模型的训练稳定是当前 RL 后训练的最大工程痛点之一。这个等价性发现把"玄学调参"变成了有理论坐标的旋钮。

灵感来源

📰 Industry

What happened
Just two weeks after its first open-source model Inkling, Mira Murati's Thinking Machines released Inkling-Small — a 276B-total / 12B-active-parameter multimodal reasoning MoE under Apache 2.0. It beats its larger sibling on several benchmarks, trails by less than one point overall, and its NVFP4 checkpoint runs on a single NVIDIA B300 GPU.
Why it matters
This is the first direct validation in the open-source community that "a quarter of the size, near-equal performance" is achievable for frontier-class MoEs. For self-hosting and private-deployment teams, frontier capability is now within single-GPU budget.
Connections
DeepSeek-V4-Flash also pits 13B active parameters against a 428B model the same day — the efficiency race is replacing the parameter arms race in open source.
What happened
DeepSeek published V4-Flash-0731 on Hugging Face and moved its official API to public beta, explicitly marking it as the official release superseding the preview. Architecture and size are unchanged — the gains come from re-post-training, not a new design. The 13B-active MoE scores 82.7 on Terminal-Bench, ranks ahead of 428B MiniMax M3 on Artificial Analysis, and is priced at $0.14/$0.27 per million tokens with a 98% cache-hit discount, released under MIT.
Why it matters
As Simon Willison put it, this may be the best value-per-intelligence model on the market today. For agent developers, the cost structure has materially changed — long-context, cache-heavy agent workloads can drop by roughly two orders of magnitude.
Connections
llama.cpp added MTP/DSpark support for V4-Flash the same day — the local-inference ecosystem is keeping pace.

Unlike the existing Workspace Agents, Presence targets external scenarios, and OpenAI's own engineers step in for complex cases.
Why it matters
This marks OpenAI's shift from "selling models" to "selling production-ready agents." For teams struggling with agent reliability in production, it signals that agent operations are becoming a cloud-vendor-level service rather than something every company must build in-house.

Why it matters
Long-horizon reliability is the biggest bottleneck for agents in production. Meta's approach requires no changes to the main model — just add a lightweight memory agent. It's an immediately replicable engineering pattern.

📄 Papers

What happened
Existing token compression for omnimodal models typically lets one modality dictate what to keep in others — an assumption that often breaks: for the same query, audio and video relevance peak at different moments. OmniScope anchors on the query and compresses each modality independently, training-free.
Why it matters
The cost bottleneck of long-video/multimodal contexts is token count. OmniScope's training-free approach can plug directly into existing inference pipelines — a low-effort, high-value engineering direction.
What happened
The paper identifies a structural source of OPSD's brittleness: vanilla OPSD is precisely the β=1 member of a broader policy-optimization family, where β weights the KL penalty anchoring the student to a reference policy. Making β a controllable regularizer markedly improves training stability.
Why it matters
Reasoning-model training stability is one of the biggest engineering pain points in RL post-training. This equivalence turns murky hyperparameter tuning into a knob with theoretical coordinates.
What happened
A controlled study compares lexical, dense, graph-based, and agentic retrieval across 28 strictly nested corpus tiers spanning ~450x in scale. The verdict is blunt: under one reader and one question set, BM25 lexical retrieval shows striking cost-performance advantages as scale grows.
Why it matters
