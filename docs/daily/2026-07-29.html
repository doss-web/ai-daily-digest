What happened
Anthropic published a blog post detailing how its latest model, Claude Mythos Preview, with system prompt guidance, successfully discovered mathematical weaknesses in HAWK-256 (a post-quantum signature scheme) and a reduced-round AES-128 (7 rounds). 📄 Papers

What happened
Moonshot AI released the technical paper for Kimi K3, detailing the design of the 2.8T-parameter MoE: Kimi Delta Attention (improving information flow across sequence length), Gated MLA, Stable LatentMoE (activating 16/896 experts per token), etc. The paper claims a 2.5× improvement in pretraining efficiency and open-sources infrastructure.
Why it matters
This is a high-quality engineering report of an open frontier model, an invaluable technical reference for teams researching large-scale MoE training and inference optimization.
What happened
Researchers propose WorldDiT, a unified diffusion transformer architecture that simultaneously generates continuous action chunks and predicts RGB patch targets from future video frames. It achieves strong baseline performance on multiple robot manipulation benchmarks without requiring a large pretrained VLM as the action backbone.
Why it matters
This is the first time a diffusion model unifies world modeling and action generation, offering significant implications for embodied AI — potentially moving away from reliance on massive VLMs toward more efficient end-to-end learning.
What happened
The paper proposes an extreme approach: freeze a 12B model and maintain a persistent memory of verified solutions around it. Once a problem family is independently verified, new instances are answered with zero generation tokens, bit-exact. Achieves 100% accuracy on 180 fresh instances with zero generated tokens.
Why it matters
Given rising inference costs and hallucination issues, this "memory + verification" paradigm offers an alternative. Valuable for teams deploying high-determinacy tasks (e.g., compliance, math).
What happened
TILT is a training-free test-time alignment method that uses the model's intrinsic reward (not an external reward model) to correct compositional generation failures (e.g., "red cube on the left of a blue sphere"). It significantly improves compositional text-to-image quality on multiple benchmarks.
Why it matters
A direct improvement to diffusion model compositionality without retraining; can be plugged into existing pipelines. Useful for developers using Stable Diffusion and similar models.
What happened
The paper proposes "program distillation" — distilling an LLM judge's decision logic into inspectable and editable programs, replacing per-call LLM evaluation. This significantly reduces cost and latency while providing transparent, interpretable evaluations.
Why it matters
LLM-as-a-judge has become a standard step in AI development, but it's expensive and opaque. Program distillation offers a scalable alternative with direct engineering value for teams that need frequent evaluation (training, model selection, quality monitoring).
